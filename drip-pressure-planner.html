How this is calculated
Tree count — area (m²) ÷ spacing². Emitters — trees × emitters per tree. Demand — emitters × emitter flow.
Required source pressure — emitter pressure + friction loss − elevation assist, where friction loss is distance ÷ 100 × mainline factor and elevation assist is elevation (m) × 0.0981 bar/m. A negative elevation (source below the field) adds to the requirement instead.
Booster head, if pressure is short — shortfall (bar) ÷ 0.0981, converted to metres of pump head.
Zones — ceil(demand ÷ available source flow), i.e. how many sections you must irrigate one after another rather than all at once.
Buffer tank — demand (L/hr) × run time × dry days — how much stored water keeps the same schedule running if the source fails in the dry season.
These are field-planning rules of thumb, not a certified hydraulic design — enough to size a pilot and know what to ask a supplier for.
Defaults reflect the interview with Tatae (2.5 ha, no sprinklers, unmeasured source). Adjust any field to model another farm.
