Drip System Schematic

Drawn in P&ID convention — the notation water engineers actually use for pipe, valve and instrument diagrams. Everything downstream of the source (filter, regulator, zoning, trees) is identical either way; only one component changes depending on whether the water source sits above or below the field.

Source configuration

Pumplifts water when the source sits below the field
Check valvestops water flowing back toward the source
Filterscreens debris that would clog the drip emitters
Pressure regulatorsteps mainline pressure down to what drip line needs
Instrumentpressure (P) and flow (F) gauges for monitoring
Zone valveopens one section at a time when flow is limited
Pi controllerautomates valves & pump by weather and season; see its schematic
