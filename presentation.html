Keisuke

The pilot study: pest and disease control

Two organic-only lines of defense, run alongside the water system.

1

Pest control: deploy insects that eat the pests

Release beneficial predators against the snails and other pests. DOST-PCAARRD is already mass-rearing red weaver ants and lynx spiders as biological control agents for cacao insect pests in the Philippines, and weaver ants have been shown to cut pest damage in cacao.[15]

2

Disease control: keep the trees watered and healthy

Healthy, regularly watered plants are less susceptible to disease: water stress measurably raises susceptibility, and the effect reverses once regular watering resumes. The drip system and the Pi controller keep trees out of that danger zone year-round.[16]

Both measures are organic-compatible, satisfying Muravah's hard rule: no synthetic pesticides.

Where the ₱95K starter figure comes from

One farm, one year, at 2025–26 Philippine market prices.

Drip starter zone, 0.25 ha: lines, emitters, filter, regulator[13]₱30,000
Two 1,000 L poly tanks (₱3,500–7,500 each) + stand and fittings[12]₱14,000
Raspberry Pi controller kit: Pi Zero 2 W, soil-moisture, temperature/humidity, rain and tank-level sensors, relay + solenoid valves, enclosure and power[14]₱8,000
Bio-spray inputs for a year: molasses, amino ferment, herbal materials[1]₱12,000
Printed treatment calendars, checklists, ledgers₱4,000
Monitoring visits, twice monthly for a year (allowance at the ₱435/day regional wage + transport)[11]₱15,000
Training day + ~15% contingency₱12,000
Total≈ ₱95,000

Every line has a cheaper local substitute; the drip zone and the controller kit are the only equipment purchases.
